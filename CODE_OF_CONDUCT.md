# Code of conduct

muru-hosting is a small proof of concept run by one maintainer, alongside
Muru, and the same expectations apply as for
[Muru](https://github.com/muruhq/muru/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Expectations

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.

- Be clear and specific. A well stated requirement or a reproducible
  problem is the most helpful thing anyone can send.

- Be patient. The project is maintained in the time available, and not
  every request can be accepted or answered quickly.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and discussions
that do not meet these expectations, and may block people who repeatedly
ignore them.
